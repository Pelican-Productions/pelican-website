---
question: 'Can we use Sports Vouchers?'
topic: 'classes'
order: 7
---

Yes. Pelican is a Sports Vouchers Plus provider. Children in Reception to Year 9
receive 2 x $100 vouchers per calendar year, and every Pelican program is
eligible.
<a href="https://www.sportsvouchers.sa.gov.au/2026-Sports-Voucher-Plus-Voucher-Form.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Fill
in the voucher form</a> or visit the
<a href="https://www.sportsvouchers.sa.gov.au/how-to-use-voucher/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sports
Vouchers Plus website</a>.

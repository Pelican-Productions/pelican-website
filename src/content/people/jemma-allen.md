- Trained in musical theatre at Jason Coleman’s Ministry of Dance, the Elder
  Conservatorium of Music and the Australian Company of Performing Arts, after
  starting out with a jazz background.
- Resident vocal tutor at Pelican Productions, and a vocal tutor on Music
  Theatre Camp.
- Has lectured in musical theatre, acting through song, audition preparation and
  ensemble singing at GWB Studios.
- Mentor and co-director for the Hive Inspire cabaret program.
- Stage credits include Chicago, Ivy + Bean the Musical and the Australian tour
  of Reclaiming Harry, and she is one third of the award-winning comedy cabaret
  trio Bisexual Intellectuals.
- Has directed, choreographed and musically directed shows including RENT,
  Urinetown, Chitty Chitty Bang Bang, The Addams Family, Legally Blonde and 13!
  The Musical.

- Bachelor of Music (Voice) and Diploma of Music, plus diplomas in further
  disciplines (DipCHyp, DipRT, DipNLP, DipPsych, DipGST).
- More than 25 years’ experience in music theatre performance and vocal
  education.
- Teaches across a wide range of musical styles, guiding singers toward healthy,
  expressive and sustainable voice use.
- Her approach considers every part of the person that can affect the voice,
  including physical health, stress, energy and lived experience.
- Co-founder of Pelican Productions and The Nest Studios, and Course Director of
  Pelican Pro.

- Studying a Bachelor of Music (Advanced) (Music Education) at Adelaide
  University’s Elder Conservatorium, specialising in musical theatre
  composition.
- Resident vocal tutor at Pelican Productions’ Academy classes.
- Directs Reo Gerhardy Musical Tuition, teaching vocal and piano lessons at
  local primary and secondary schools with VocaLab accreditation.
- Emerging composer with Home Grown Musicals Australia, who adapted Markus
  Zusak’s _The Book Thief_ under Adelaide Symphony Orchestra mentorship at
  seventeen.
- Compositions have been performed at the Adelaide Fringe, Home Grown’s
  interstate showcases and Launceston’s Australian Music Theatre Festival.
- Commissioned to create a musical adaptation of A.F. Harrold’s _The Song from
  Somewhere Else_ with Bloomsbury Publishing.
- Music director for the Australian professional tour of _Footloose_ (Assisting
  Work), _Nerdy Prudes Must Die_, _Legally Blonde JR_, _High School Musical:
  Onstage_ and _Mary Poppins_ (Rehearsal Pianist).

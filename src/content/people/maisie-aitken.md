- Recent graduate of the Elder Conservatorium with a Bachelor of Music in Jazz
  Performance.
- Awarded the Generations in Jazz Vocal Scholarship and the Helpmann Academy
  Jazz Award for Voice in 2025.
- Recognised as the 2026 Undergraduate College Vocal Jazz Soloist (Outstanding
  Performance) in the International DownBeat Student Music Awards.
- Completed Estill Voice Training Level 1 & 2 Certification, and applies these
  vocal health techniques in her teaching.
- Has studied singing with Rosie Hosking since 2018.
- Pelican since 2015: her first musical was Chitty Chitty Bang Bang at age 10,
  followed by mid year productions, Music Theatre Camps and a 2018 trip to New
  York for iTheatrics.

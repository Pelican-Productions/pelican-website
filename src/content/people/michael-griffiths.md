- Graduated from WAAPA with a Bachelor in Musical Theatre in 1999, and holds
  Estill Voice Level 1 & 2 certification.
- Won the 2016 Helpmann Award for Best Cabaret Performer, and Best Cabaret at
  the 2014 Adelaide Fringe.
- Stage credits include Jersey Boys (Green Room Award nominee), SHOUT!, We Will
  Rock You and Priscilla Queen of the Desert.
- Has performed at New York’s 54 Below, London’s Royal Albert Hall and the
  Sydney Opera House.
- Artistic Director of the Perth International Cabaret Festival, with a star on
  the Adelaide Festival Centre’s Walk of Fame.
- Teaches at GWB Studios, Pelican Productions and Pembroke School, and has led
  the Adelaide Festival Centre’s Class of Cabaret.
